Founder’s story

“Two friends missed their grandmother’s filter coffee. So they opened a café.”

Led by where it all started. It feels personal, warm, and hard to copy, because nobody else has this story.

Vox: start with the people.
Competitive market

“The only café on this street that roasts its own beans.”

Led by what makes it different from everyone nearby. Sharp, specific, easy to remember.

Vox: say what nobody else can.
Industry rules

“Every bean on our menu, traced back to the farm it came from.”

Led by what they can prove. No big claims, no “best in town”. Just facts people can check.

Vox: only promise what’s true.
